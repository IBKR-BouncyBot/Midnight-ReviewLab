ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PROOFS

Show that the rules hold.

The contract checks credential membership, expertise, validity and duplicate prevention. Private secrets stay outside the public review receipt. On testnet, a real prover supplies the cryptographic proof.

NIGHT → DUST

Plan for repeat participation.

Registered NIGHT generates DUST, the resource consumed by Midnight transaction fees. A future sponsor can cover reviewers’ network fees. This testnet client uses your wallet’s test DUST.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Keep three budgets separate.

DUST covers blockchain fees. Expert compensation and AI computing need their own funding. The demo records review approvals and entitlements; it does not hold or distribute real rewards.

Explore the DUST scenario calculator →

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE TESTING

A research prototype.

Demo credentials do not establish real professional qualifications. A production marketplace needs trusted issuers, independent review-quality checks and a payment service. A valid review transaction does not prove that the feedback is correct.

Local contract execution, proof generation and accepted network transactions are separate milestones. A saved receipt can be internally consistent without establishing authentic credentials, a complete history or blockchain acceptance.
